## Style prompts

1. Flat: crisp vector-style illustration, solid color fields, no texture, outlines, 3D or gradients. Clear elegant silhouette.
2. Line: refined minimal monoline, consistent cobalt/pink outlines, white interiors, small cyan/pink accents. No shading or dimensionality.
3. Soft 3D: premium matte clay-style render, rounded bevels, real thickness, tactile material, gentle ambient contact shadows, approachable and sophisticated.
4. Glass: translucent cyan-blue foreground and pink rear glass, transparent overlap, controlled refraction, polished bevels, readable on white.
5. Paper: layered colored cardstock, fine paper fibers, crisp thin cut edges, raised geometric details, delicate contact shadows, handmade precision.
6. Grain: editorial screenprint, slightly irregular confident shapes, fine ink grain, subtle offset print texture, flat aesthetic, no 3D.
